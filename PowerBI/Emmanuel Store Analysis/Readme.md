
# Emmanuel Store Sales Analysis Dashboard
## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents a comprehensive sales performance analysis for Emmanuel Store using an interactive dashboard developed in Microsoft Power BI.
The dashboard provides insights into revenue generation, profitability, product performance, customer purchasing behavior, and monthly sales trends. The analysis helps identify top-performing products and customers while uncovering opportunities to improve sales strategy and inventory planning.

## 📍 Objectives
-	Monitor overall sales revenue and profitability
-	Track monthly sales and profit trends
-	Analyze product performance based on units sold and revenue contribution
-	Identify high-value customers and purchasing patterns
-	Evaluate quantity sold across products and customer segments
-	Support business decisions through data-driven insights

## 📊 Dataset Overview
The dataset contains transactional sales records including:
-	Customer names
-	Product names
-	Revenue generated
-	Profit earned
-	Quantity sold
-	Purchase month
The dashboard aggregates and visualizes sales activities to measure overall business performance.

## 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
-	**Total Revenue:** $87.95k
-	**Total Profit:** $43.21k
-	**Total Quantity Sold:** 3,001 Units

# 🔍 Detailed Insights
## 🛒 Product Performance Analysis
### Top-Selling Products by Units Sold
The dashboard reveals that survival and utility products generated the highest sales volume (Units Sold):
-	** Multi Survival Knife:** 477
-	** Nylon Rope:** 390
-	**Duct Tape:** 365
-	**Stainless Axe:** 350
-	** N95 Mask:** 343
### Revenue & Profit Contribution by Product
Several products generated strong revenue while maintaining healthy profit margins:
-	The **Multi-tool Survival Knife** produced approximately **$21K revenue** and **$13K profit**, making it the highest-performing product overall.
-	**Nylon Rope** contributed around **$14K revenue** and **$9K profit**.
-	**Stainless Axe** generated approximately **$16K revenue** and **$5K profit**. 
-	Lower-performing products such as **Water Pack**, **Solar Battery**, and **Duct Tape** generated smaller revenue contributions but still maintained profitability.
### Product Insights
-	Survival and emergency-preparedness products dominate customer demand.
-	High-margin products contribute significantly to profitability despite lower sales volumes in some categories.
-	Frequently purchased utility items indicate recurring customer needs and practical usage trends.

# 📅 Monthly Sales Trend Analysis
## Units Sold by Month
Sales volume experienced a noticeable decline across the analyzed months:
-	**January:** 1,270
-	**February:** 1,122
-	**March:** 609
### Trend Insights
-	January recorded the strongest sales performance.
-	Sales volume declined significantly by March, potentially indicating:

•	Seasonal demand fluctuations
•	Inventory limitations
•	Reduced customer activity
•	Market slowdown

## Profit Trend by Month
-	**March:** $8K
-	**February:** $15K
-	**January:** $19K
**Total:** $43K
### Profit Insights
-	January delivered the highest monthly profitability.
-	Profitability remained positive throughout all periods.
-	Despite declining sales volume, the business maintained strong profit margins.

# 📊 Business Performance Summary
### Key Findings
-	The store achieved strong profitability with nearly **49% profit relative to revenue**.
-	Survival and emergency products are the primary revenue drivers.
-	Customer concentration suggests dependence on a few major buyers.
-	Sales trends indicate the need to monitor demand consistency across periods.
-	The business demonstrates effective pricing and margin management.

# 📌 Recommendations
### Sales & Marketing
-	Introduce promotional campaigns during lower-performing months.
-	Strengthen customer loyalty programs for top buyers.
-	Expand marketing around high-performing survival products.
### Inventory Management
-	Increase stock availability for fast-moving products like survival knives and nylon ropes.
-	Monitor slow-moving inventory to optimize storage costs.
### Customer Strategy
-	Develop targeted offers for repeat customers.
-	Diversify customer acquisition efforts to reduce dependency on a small customer base.
### Profit Optimization
-	Focus on products with the strongest revenue-to-profit ratios.
-	Bundle complementary products to increase average order value.

The Emmanuel Store Sales Analysis dashboard provides valuable insights into sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, and product profitability. The findings show strong overall profitability, high-performing customer segments, and consistent demand for survival-related products.
