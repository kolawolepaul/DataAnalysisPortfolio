# Robbin Company Sales Analysis (2015 - 2017)

## 📌 Overview
With the aim to find trends in revenue, profit, product categories, client demographics, and regional performance, this research examines Robbin Company's sales performance from 2015 to 2017.

A Power BI interactive dashboard was created to assist stakeholders in tracking business expansion, assessing profitability, and assisting with strategic sales decisions.

##  📍 Objectives
 - Analyze sales performance across occupations and regions
 - Track total revenue, profit, and quantity sold over multiple years
 - Identify top-performing product categories
 - Compare revenue contribution by gender
 - Monitor yearly business growth and return trends

##  📊 Dataset
 - Source: Company sales dataset
 - Timeframe: 2015 – 2017
 - Key Fields: Revenue, Profit, Quantity Sold, Product Category, Occupation, Region, Gender, Returns.
 - Business Areas Covered: Sales performance, customer segmentation, regional analysis, and product trends.

##  📈 Key Metrics (KPIs)
 - **Total Revenue:** $25M
 - **Total Profit:** $10.46M
 - **Total Quantity Sold:** 84K
 - **Total Returns:** 1,828

##  🔍 Insights
 - The company generated a total revenue of **$25 million** with an overall profit of **$10.46 million** during the analyzed period.
 - **Bikes** dominated sales contribution, generating approximately **94.89%** of total revenue, while accessories and clothing contributed smaller shares.
 - Revenue contribution by gender was nearly balanced:
	**Female:** 50.23%
	**Male:** 49.14%
	**Unspecified:** 0.63%
 - Customers from the **Professional occupation group** purchased the highest quantity of products **(27K units)** and placed the largest number of orders **(8K orders)**.
 - **Australia** emerged as the top-performing region with revenue exceeding $7.4M, followed by Southwest and Northwest regions.
 - Sales performance improved significantly between **2015 and 2016,** with revenue increasing from **$6.4M to $9.3M**.
 - Product quantity sold increased consistently over the years, reaching approximately **43K units in 2017**.
 -	Profit growth stabilized after 2016, remaining close to **$4M** through 2017.

## 🗾 Regional Revenue Highlights
 - **Australia:** $7.42M
 - **Southwest:** $4.82M
 - **Northwest:** $3.10M
 - **United Kingdom:** $2.90M
 - **Germany:** $2.52M

## 🛠 Tools 
 - Power BI — Dashboard creation & visualization
 - Microsoft Excel — Data preparation & cleaning

## 📌 Conclusion
The dashboard reveals strong business growth driven primarily by bike sales and high-performing regions such as Australia and Southwest. Customer purchasing behavior indicates professionals are the most valuable customer segment, while yearly trends show substantial revenue and quantity growth from 2015 to 2017. These insights can support inventory planning, targeted marketing strategies, and regional sales optimization.
